How verification works

New profiles start as “verification pending”.

In this milestone verification is a flag in the data, not a real government check. Your ID number is only checked for format in your browser and the server. Only the last 4 characters are stored, never the full number.

Demo project: please do not enter a real Aadhaar number. A sample such as 234567890123 passes the format check.
