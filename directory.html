How the trust score works

A 0–100 score built from four visible parts.

  • 30 · ID check flagAwarded when the profile is marked verified.
  • 30 · RatingAverage rating out of 5, scaled to 30 points.
  • 10 · ExperienceOne point per year of experience, up to 10.
  • 30 · Record reliabilityShare of reviewed ledger entries that the helper confirmed rather than disputed. Helpers with no reviewed entries get a neutral 15.

Ratings and verification in this milestone are sample data to demonstrate the interface. They are not real reviews or government checks.
